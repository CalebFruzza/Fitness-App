Exercise & Calorie Tracker

Build Muscle

Strength training and weightlifting help build lean muscle, which increases your resting metabolic rate and burns more calories throughout the day.

Cardio Exercises

Running, cycling, and swimming are excellent cardio workouts that burn high calories in a short time. Aim for at least 150 minutes per week.

Diet & Exercise

Weight loss requires both a calorie deficit and regular exercise. Combine cardio workouts with strength training for optimal results.

High Intensity

HIIT (High-Intensity Interval Training) is one of the most effective ways to burn calories quickly and improve cardiovascular fitness.

Consistency Wins

Exercise 3-5 times per week and maintain a healthy diet. Consistency is more important than intensity for long-term weight loss.

Start Slow

If you're new to exercise, start with walking or light cycling. Gradually increase intensity to avoid injury and build sustainable habits.
